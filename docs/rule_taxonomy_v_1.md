# COMMFACT Rule Taxonomy v1.0

> **Purpose**  
This document defines the **rule taxonomy** used in COMMFACT Lite v1.0.  
It explains how rules are categorised, why they exist, and how they function within the governance system.

> **Scope note**  
This taxonomy supports **risk identification and decision accountability**, not automatic compliance or legal judgement.

---

## 1. Taxonomy Design Principles

The COMMFACT rule taxonomy is designed based on the following principles:

1. **Normative-first**  
   Rules originate from regulatory, ethical, and professional norms — not from linguistic patterns alone.

2. **Risk-based, not binary**  
   Rules flag potential risk categories; they do not declare content lawful or unlawful.

3. **Human-centred governance**  
   Rules exist to support human judgement, not replace it.

4. **Explainability over coverage**  
   Each rule must be explainable to non-technical reviewers in plain language.

---

## 2. Top-Level Rule Categories

COMMFACT v1.0 defines three top-level governance rule categories.

### 2.1 Claim Integrity Rules
Rules addressing **truthfulness, proportionality, and substantiation** of claims.

Focus:
- Preventing misleading certainty
- Avoiding exaggerated outcomes
- Ensuring responsible expectation-setting

These rules are particularly critical in regulated or high-trust communication contexts.

---

### 2.2 Comparative & Persuasive Influence Rules
Rules addressing **comparative positioning and persuasive dominance** that may distort audience judgement.

Focus:
- Unsubstantiated superiority
- Implicit dominance claims
- Manipulative framing

These rules often require contextual assessment and justification rather than outright rejection.

---

### 2.3 Outcome Guarantee & Assurance Rules
Rules addressing **explicit or implicit guarantees** of results, success, or impact.

Focus:
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Certainty of success
- Removal of uncertainty or variability

This category carries the **highest governance risk** and strictest review expectations.

## 3. Rule Inventory (v1.0)

### LGL-ABS-01 — Absolute Claim
**Category:** Claim Integrity  
**Severity:** HIGH

Addresses claims that present outcomes or conditions as absolute or unconditional.

---

### LGL-SUP-01 — Superiority Claim
**Category:** Comparative & Persuasive Influence  
**Severity:** MEDIUM–HIGH

Addresses claims asserting superiority or dominance without verifiable reference.

---

### LGL-GUA-01 — Guarantee Claim
**Category:** Outcome Guarantee & Assurance  
**Severity:** HIGH

Addresses claims that guarantee results, outcomes, or success.

---

## 4. Severity Philosophy

Severity levels in COMMFACT reflect **governance risk**, not moral judgement.

- **HIGH**  
  Claims with strong regulatory, reputational, or ethical exposure.  
  These cannot be auto-approved and always require explicit justification.

- **MEDIUM–HIGH**  
  Claims with contextual risk that may be defensible if properly substantiated and documented.

Severity does not determine outcomes; it determines **review strictness**.

---

## 5. What This Taxonomy Does NOT Include

To maintain clarity and governance integrity, v1.0 intentionally excludes:

- Style or tone preferences
- Brand voice alignment
- Grammar or copy-editing rules
- Sentiment optimisation
- Recommendation or rewrite guidance

These exclusions are deliberate and foundational to COMMFACT’s positioning.
